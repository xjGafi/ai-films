### 1.2 色彩标注系统分镜板 — @aimikoda

**来源**: https://x.com/aimikoda/status/2053053922332262547

**特点**：使用颜色编码系统分离不同类型的标注，避免视频模型混淆。

```
Create a raw contemporary dance performance storyboard focused on intense
physical movement and live singing. Use reference image for the character.

16:9 storyboard sheet, 12 cinematic panels. The actual storyboard drawings
must be black and white only: rough pencil lines, minimal detail, fast gesture
drawing energy, simple anatomy construction and strong silhouette readability.
Keep the artwork lightweight, dynamic and unfinished like early choreography previs.

A solitary female performer sings continuously while executing an emotionally
charged contemporary dance routine inside a massive empty brutalist hall.
The choreography is aggressive, fluid and constantly evolving: rapid turns,
floor slides, crawling transitions, sharp body isolations, trembling hands,
extreme balance shifts, hair whips, lunges, jumps, collapsing movements and
distorted sculptural poses.

Every panel must contain visible motion and strong body momentum. Avoid static
standing poses. The performer should feel trapped between ritual, exhaustion
and emotional release.

Use cinematic arthouse camerawork with handheld energy, whip pans, orbit
movement, overhead shots, side silhouettes, aggressive close-ups, long lens
compression and extreme negative space.

Keep the environment minimal: empty space, smoke, fabric motion, harsh light
beams and wet floor reflections only.

Annotation color system:
- red arrows = body movement
- blue arrows = camera movement
- green marks = framing / composition notes
- orange marks = lighting direction
- purple marks = vocal / emotional emphasis
- black text = short lens notes and panel labels

No timestamps. End with one overwhelming final movement pose beneath a harsh
isolated spotlight.
```

**关键技巧提取**：
- **色彩标注系统**是核心创新：不同颜色 = 不同指令类型
- 分镜图本身保持黑白素描，标注用彩色叠加
- 明确要求"避免静态站立姿势"
- "like early choreography previs" 定义了sketch的完成度

### 2.1 基于分镜的完整视频 prompt — @aimikoda

**来源**: https://x.com/aimikoda/status/2053053922332262547

**特点**：使用 IPA（国际音标）控制口型 + FACS（面部动作编码系统）控制表情。

```
Use the storyboard reference @[storyboard ref] as the complete visual and
choreography source for a 15-second video. Follow all 12 beats sequentially
from left to right, top to bottom. Do not reinterpret the actions, poses,
camera angles or emotional progression. Preserve the storyboard's shot order,
movement logic, framing variety and final pose.

Compress the full 12-beat sequence into 15 seconds. Each beat must appear
clearly as a fast motion snapshot, not as a full-length action. Use urgent
rhythm, quick cuts, match cuts and whip transitions. No pauses until the
final beat.

Keep the same single female performer @[character ref] identity from the
character reference. Do not duplicate her. Continuous live singing throughout,
with visible breath, mouth movement, body strain, fabric motion, floor contact
and emotional escalation.

Environment: massive empty brutalist hall, wet reflective floor, smoke, harsh
light beams, minimal space. Cinematic arthouse camera energy: handheld movement,
close-ups, overhead flashes, low angles, orbit motion and extreme negative space.

Final beat holds for the last 1 seconds under one harsh isolated spotlight.

Lyrics only once,

IPA:
/aɪ muːv θruː ˈsaɪləns/
/ðə laɪt breɪks miː/

FACS: AU1+AU4 tension, AU5 intensity, AU25 singing mouth, AU26 open release,
AU43 exhausted blink.

No text, no annotations, no timestamps, no watermark.
```

**关键技巧提取**：
- **"Follow all 12 beats sequentially from left to right, top to bottom"** — 明确告诉模型读取分镜的顺序
- **"Do not reinterpret"** — 防止模型自由发挥
- **"Compress the full 12-beat sequence into 15 seconds"** — 明确压缩指令
- **IPA 音标**：控制口型同步的精确度
- **FACS 编码**：AU1+AU4=紧张皱眉, AU5=眼睛睁大, AU25=嘴唇分开, AU26=下巴张开, AU43=疲惫闭眼
- **"Each beat must appear clearly as a fast motion snapshot, not as a full-length action"** — 节奏控制

• Low angle on Kevin's entrance — make him look massive

### 4.2 20-shot 煎饼教程（2段×10shot） — @Dheepanratnam

**来源**: https://x.com/Dheepanratnam/status/2053202489164337379

**核心方法**：将 30s 视频拆为两个 15s 片段，每段 10 shot，每 shot 约 1.5s。

#### Part 1 — 前 15 秒 (Shots 1-10)

```
Classic Pancake Stack — First 15 Seconds
Use the attached CLASSIC PANCAKE STACK storyboard image as the main reference.

IMPORTANT:
Animate ONLY PART 1 — the TOP HALF of the storyboard.
Do not animate Part 2 in this clip.
This is the FIRST 15 SECONDS of a 30-second pancake tutorial.
Follow the sequence exactly in order from shots 1 to 10.
Do not skip, reorder, merge, or invent steps.

GOAL:
Create a beautiful 15-second cooking tutorial video that brings the TOP HALF
of the storyboard to life as a premium food video.

STYLE:
High-quality stylized 3D cooking video, bright premium brunch aesthetic,
warm natural kitchen lighting, clean white and wood tones, appetizing golden
food rendering, glossy batter texture, realistic steam and pan heat, charming
chef performance. Keep the same young male chef throughout: expressive face,
dark brown wavy hair, white chef jacket with black buttons, dark apron.

AUDIO:
No background music. Use environment and cooking sounds only:
light kitchen room tone, whisking, bowl handling, butter sizzling, batter
pouring, pancake sizzling, spatula movement, soft cooking ambience.
No voiceover. No lyrics. No music.

TIMING:
15 seconds total. 10 shots total. About 1.5 seconds per shot.
Use clean, quick cuts. Each shot should show one clear action.

SHOT ORDER — FOLLOW EXACTLY:
SHOT 1 — INGREDIENTS READY (展示食材)
SHOT 2 — DRY MIX (干料入碗)
SHOT 3 — WET MIX (湿料入碗)
SHOT 4 — WHISK BATTER (搅拌面糊)
SHOT 5 — HEAT PAN (热锅化黄油)
SHOT 6 — POUR BATTER (倒面糊)
SHOT 7 — BUBBLES FORM (气泡形成特写)
SHOT 8 — FLIP (翻面)
SHOT 9 — GOLDEN FINISH (金黄色完成面)
SHOT 10 — SET ASIDE (转移到盘中)

NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS:
Do not animate the bottom half / Part 2 in this clip.
Do not add syrup, berries, powdered sugar, or final plating yet.
Do not change the chef design. Do not add extra characters.
Do not add text overlays. Do not add music. Do not skip steps.
Do not show the final pancake stack yet.

END STATE:
End with several cooked pancakes ready to be stacked, so the next clip
can continue naturally.
```

#### Part 2 — 后 15 秒 (Shots 11-20)

```
Classic Pancake Stack — Second 15 Seconds
Use the attached CLASSIC PANCAKE STACK storyboard image as the main reference.

IMPORTANT:
Animate ONLY PART 2 — the BOTTOM HALF of the storyboard.
This is the SECOND 15 SECONDS of a 30-second pancake tutorial.
Follow the sequence exactly in order from shots 11 to 20.

VERY IMPORTANT CONTINUITY NOTE:
This clip must feel like a direct continuation of Part 1.
Start with the cooked pancakes already prepared from the previous clip.
Maintain the same chef, same kitchen, same lighting, same countertop,
same plate style, and same food appearance.
The two clips must join seamlessly when edited together.

SHOT ORDER — FOLLOW EXACTLY:
SHOT 11 — BUILD STACK (叠煎饼)
SHOT 12 — ADD BUTTER (放黄油)
SHOT 13 — SYRUP POUR START (开始倒糖浆)
SHOT 14 — SYRUP CASCADE (糖浆流淌特写)
SHOT 15 — ADD BERRIES (摆浆果)
SHOT 16 — DUST SUGAR (撒糖粉)
SHOT 17 — FINAL TOUCH (最后点缀)
SHOT 18 — FORK LIFT (叉子切入)
SHOT 19 — HERO CLOSE-UP (美食特写)
SHOT 20 — PRESENTATION (端盘展示)

[其余格式同 Part 1]
```

**关键技巧**：
- **IMPORTANT / VERY IMPORTANT CONTINUITY NOTE** — 大写强调指令防止模型忽略
- **"Do not skip, reorder, merge, or invent steps"** — 四重约束
- **"Animate ONLY PART 1 — the TOP HALF"** — 明确物理位置对应
- **NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS 段落** — 详细列出所有不该做的事
- **END STATE** — 明确定义末尾状态，为下段做铺垫
- **CONTINUITY NOTE** — 跨段连续性是独立段落强调的

### 4.4 超写实科幻时装 — @ivanka_humeniuk

**来源**: https://x.com/ivanka_humeniuk/status/2053655977484775892

**特点**：10 个镜头/15 秒的极高密度节奏，超详细物理和摄影描述。

```
Keyword & Overall Intent: Hyperrealism at the intersection of sci-fi desert
cinematic style and avant-garde haute couture. A relentless sandstorm becomes
the runway for a legion of female models clad in architecturally inspired
haute couture, their icy aura piercing the storm's eye.

Focus on ultra-detailed textures: sand clinging to damp skin, scratched chrome
metal, heavy leather. Wild camera movement, obscuring orange dust particles,
and realistic physics dominate all action.

Shot 1 (0-1.5s) Macro (MS) • Rapid zoom
Extreme macro close-up of a massive, mirrored half-face mask, revealing
minute scratches caused by impact of sand. The mask reflects an approaching
wall of orange dust. Camera pulls back sharply, revealing five female models
in heavy architecturally inspired leather trench coats.

Shot 2 (1.5-3s) Close-up (CU) • Rapid panning
Camera pans to female model with thick silver dreadlocks. Focus on her neck:
hyper-realistic beads of sweat, golden grains of sand instantly adhering.
She stares straight ahead without blinking.

Shot 3 (3-4.5s) Medium shot (MS) • Handheld
Violent camera shake, thick orange sand cloud completely obscuring the lens,
creating a natural matching edit. A new female model suddenly emerges from
the sand cloud, wearing complex transparent plastic breathing mask.

Shot 4 (4.5-6s) Medium Shot (MS) • Tracking
Camera tracks the female model corps as they march forward. A huge torn canvas
flies violently toward the camera. A model wearing Kevlar armor corset deftly
flicks the heavy canvas away.

Shot 5 (6-7.5s) Close-up (CU) • Low Angle, Fixed Shot
Extremely low angle of cracked desert landscape. Heavy combat boots and high
heels with metal spikes slam into dry ground. Each impact sends up a localized
fountain of fine dust. Camera vibrates from the sheer weight.

Shot 6 (7.5-9s) Medium Shot (MS) • Circling to the right
Three female models stand atop a sand dune, holding edge of a huge dark silk
parachute. A fierce storm billows the silk like giant sails. Camera circles
beneath the taut, vibrating fabric.

Shot 7 (9-10.5s) Extreme Close-up (ECU) • Rapid panning
Female model whose lower face is tightly wrapped in heavy tactical Arabic
headscarf woven with metallic threads. She pulls it down sharply to reveal
dark full lips coated with thick layer of orange dust. Defiant cold laugh.

Shot 8 (10.5-12s) Extreme Wide Shot (EWS) • Rapid Pull-out
Epic cinematic scene. Dark silhouettes of five female models move through
dense orange dust storm. Hurricane-force winds force heavy trench coats to
lie parallel to the ground.

Shot 9 (12-13.5s) Medium Shot (MS) • Rapid Close-up
Camera zooms in rapidly on lead model wearing scratched chrome mask. She
accelerates aggressively, walking directly towards camera with predatory,
high-energy stride.

Shot 10 (13.5-15s) Macro Lens (MS) • Fixed Lens
Lead female model walks directly in front of camera. Wearing tactical gloves,
she scoops up handful of rough desert sand and violently throws it directly
at the lens. Image instantly buried. Extreme macro reveals hundreds of
individual grains sliding and scraping against the camera lens.

[每个 shot 末尾包含物理约束标注如: Rigid body collisions / Fluid movement /
Complex fabric simulation / Realistic conservation of momentum / Particle
collisions and realistic physics]
```

**关键技巧**：
- 每 shot 1.5 秒，10 shots = 15s（比其他作者更密集）
- 每个 shot 标注**镜头类型缩写** (MS/CU/ECU/EWS) + **摄像机运动** + **核心动作**
- **物理约束标签**：`Rigid body collisions`, `Complex fabric simulation`, `Particle collisions`
- **材质细节极致**：sand clinging to damp skin, scratched chrome metal
- **"obscuring the lens" / "throws sand at the lens"** — 用镜头遮挡做自然转场
- 开头有 `Keyword & Overall Intent` 总领
